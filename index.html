The Crisis We Face

Traditional Vietnamese folk games are vanishing from modern society

📱

Digital Dominance

Urban children are heavily reliant on digital entertainment, with screen time replacing active, social play in beautifully designed public spaces like the Hanoi Museum.

🏛️

Empty Museums

Local cultural institutions suffer from low engagement and attendance by domestic tourists, despite having the perfect spaces for cultural activities.

🌐

Cultural Erosion

We're losing our intangible heritage and physical traditions, along with the community cohesion that comes from active, face-to-face play.

The Impact Hits Hard

Loss of Cultural Heritage Weakened Community Bonds Missed Learning Opportunities

Why Current Approaches Fall Short

Existing initiatives aren't solving the problem effectively

1

Pedestrian Street Activations

Walking street events like Hoan Kiem Lake

THE GAP

Highly weather-dependent, chaotic, and doesn't foster deeper connection to Vietnamese history or art

2

Standard Museum Programs

Traditional guided tours and education programs

THE GAP

3

School-Based Festivals

Cultural game festivals during holidays

THE GAP

Students are only engaged for one or two days during the event duration

Our Unique Solution

Introducing the Museum Challenge Pop-Up Series

🎯 INNOVATIVE APPROACH

Transforming Museums into Dynamic Play Spaces

By creating a series of engaging folk game challenges within museums, we blend cultural heritage with active play, making museums exciting destinations for families.

🎯

Target Audience

Urban Vietnamese youth aged 8–14 and their parents

📅

Format

4-week weekend event series at a specific museum

💰

Budget

3,000,000 - 5,000,000 VND (Low cost, high impact)

📍

Scale

Pilot program at Hanoi Museum

How It Works

1

2

3

4-Week Program Timeline

Week 1: Introduction & Discovery

Launch event featuring basic games like Ô ăn quan and Nhảy dây. Focus on engaging families and introducing the concept.

Week 2: Physical & Team Challenges

Introduction of Kéo co (tug-of-war) and Ném còn. Emphasizing teamwork, physical activity, and community bonding.

Week 3: Skill & Strategy Mastery

Advanced strategies in board games, introducing local school competitions, and connecting games to specific cultural artifacts.

Week 4: The Grand Finale Festival

Culminating event with tournaments, prize ceremonies, and a showcase of traditional arts. Gathering feedback for future programs.

Featured Folk Games

Rediscover the joy of traditional Vietnamese games

⭕

Ô Ăn Quan

A traditional strategic board game that teaches counting, tactics, and patience. Played with stones and drawn squares on the ground.

Strategy
🎋

Nhảy Dây

Jump rope games that build coordination, rhythm, and social connection. Often played with rhymes and group challenges.

Physical
💪

Kéo Co

Team tug-of-war competitions that foster teamwork, strength, and community spirit. A classic festival game.

Team
⚔️

Đấu Vat

Traditional wrestling that showcases strength and technique. Rooted in Vietnamese martial arts traditions.

Athletic
🎯

Ném Còn

A throwing game where players aim to throw a ball through a bamboo circle. Tests accuracy and focus.

Skill
🐓

Đá Gà

Chicken fighting game where children balance on one leg and try to knock opponents off balance. Builds agility.

Agility

Why This Matters

Preserving our heritage for future generations

🎨 Cultural Identity

Folk games are living expressions of Vietnamese culture, carrying stories, values, and traditions passed down through generations.

👥 Community Connection

These games bring people together, fostering social bonds and creating shared experiences that strengthen communities.

🧠 Active Learning

Through play, children develop physical skills, strategic thinking, teamwork, and cultural awareness simultaneously.

🏛️ Museum Revitalization

Transforming museums into vibrant community spaces increases engagement and makes cultural institutions relevant to modern families.
